Rentora Corp.

Building the infrastructure
for renting, not buying.

Rentora Corp. is the company behind Rentora, a peer-to-peer marketplace that helps people rent tools, gear, and equipment from others nearby instead of buying things they'll use once.

Learn about us

What we're building

A trust layer for peer-to-peer rentals

🤝

The Rentora platform

A marketplace where anyone can list something they own and anyone can request to rent it — with real-time messaging, deposit handling, and reviews built in from the start.

🛡️

Trust & safety

Person-to-person star ratings, a support desk staffed by real people, and a growing set of tools to keep disputes fair for both renters and owners.

🌱

Less waste, more use

Every rental is one fewer thing bought, used twice, and forgotten in a closet. We think that adds up.

📍

Local by design

Rentora is built around nearby, in-person exchanges — real neighbors lending real things to each other.
