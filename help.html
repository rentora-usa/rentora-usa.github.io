Help Center

How can we help?

Answers to the most common questions about renting, listing, and staying safe on Rentora.

⌕

Getting started

Do I need an account to browse listings?

No — anyone can search and view listings without signing in. You'll need an account to send a rental request, message an owner, or list an item yourself.

How do I sign up?

Click the account icon in the top right and choose "Sign up." You can create an account with an email and password, or continue with Google or Apple in one tap.

Is Rentora free to use?

Yes — browsing, listing, and messaging are free. Rentora doesn't process payments yet; renters and owners currently arrange payment between themselves.

Renting an item

How do I request to rent something?

Open a listing, choose your start and end dates, and click "Request to rent." The owner will accept or decline your request from their dashboard, and you'll see the status update in yours.

Why were my dates unavailable?

Once an owner accepts a request, those dates are marked booked on the listing. If your chosen dates overlap an existing booking, Rentora will let you know before you submit the request.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes, as long as it's still pending. Go to Dashboard → "Rentals I've requested" and click "Cancel request." Once an owner accepts, cancellations should be worked out directly with them.

How do I contact the owner?

Click "Message owner" on any listing, or "Message" next to a request in your dashboard, to open a direct conversation.

Listing an item

How do I list something?

Click "List an item" in the header, fill in the details — title, description, category, price per day, location, and photos — and publish. It'll appear in search immediately.

How do I handle a rental request?

Go to Dashboard → "Requests I've received." You can accept or decline each pending request; accepting automatically blocks those dates on your listing.

Can I hide a listing without deleting it?

Yes — use "Hide" on the dashboard to take it out of search temporarily. "Show" brings it back. Deleting is permanent.

How do I edit a listing?

From your dashboard, click "Edit" on any listing to update its details, price, photos, or description.

Reviews & trust

How do star ratings work?

After a rental is marked complete, both sides can leave a star rating and short review of the other person. Your profile shows the average of every review you've received, so others can see whether you're reliable before renting with you.

Can I edit a review I left?

Yes — go back to the completed rental in your dashboard and click "Edit review." Only your original rental request can be reviewed once, and editing replaces it rather than adding a second review.

What if someone doesn't show up or an item isn't as described?

Message the other person first to try to resolve it directly. Leave an honest review afterward — that's currently the main way Rentora surfaces reliability. Formal reporting and moderation tools are on the roadmap.

Account & privacy

What can other people see about me?

Your display name, photo, bio, location text, star rating, reviews, and active listings are public on your profile. Your email and exact address are never shown — exact pickup details are meant to be shared directly once a rental is confirmed.

How do I update my profile?

Open the account menu → Settings, to change your name, bio, location, or photo.

How do I sign out?

Open the account menu in the top right and choose "Log out."
