Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Here are some things people commonly ask about Rentora.

Rentora is a marketplace where people can discover, rent, and list items. Browse available listings, find something you need, or list your own items for others to rent.
Find an item you're interested in, open its listing, and follow the rental instructions provided by the owner. Make sure you review the listing details, availability, and any requirements before requesting a rental.
Select List an item in the navigation bar and follow the steps to create your listing. You'll be able to add information, photos, pricing, and other details about your item.
Open your account and find the listing you want to manage. From there, you can update its information or remove it when appropriate.
If something goes wrong with a rental or listing, contact the Rentora support team using the form above. Include as much relevant information as possible so we can understand what happened and help resolve it.
Rentora takes account security and privacy seriously. Never share your password or sensitive account information with another person. If you believe your account may have been compromised, contact support.
Response times can vary depending on the request and how many support requests we're handling. We'll review your message and respond as soon as possible.
Use the support form above and select the appropriate category for your issue. Include what you were doing when the problem occurred and, if possible, any relevant screenshots or error messages.
